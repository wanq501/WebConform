# Curated test cases

`cases.json` holds 126 test cases with their options, the demonstrated and forbidden letters and the decisions of the eight MLLMs.
`img/` holds their screenshots, crops of the screenshots of the public Multimodal-Mind2Web test splits (osunlp/Multimodal-Mind2Web),
subject to the license of that dataset. The case figures of the paper and Fig. S1 of the supplement are drawn from these files.
